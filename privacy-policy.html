Roika Games Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Roika ("we", "us", or "our") develops and publishes mobile games. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you use our games and related services.

This Privacy Policy applies to games developed and published by Roika that link to this document, including Darkrise - Pixel Action RPG and Dice RPG (working title).

Different games may use different features and third-party services. Not every service described in this policy is necessarily used by every game.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the game and its features, we and our service providers may collect or process the following information:

  • Device information, including device model, operating system, language, and application version.
  • Technical information, such as IP address, device identifiers, and diagnostic data.
  • Gameplay information, including progress, achievements, settings, and in-game activity.
  • Advertising information, including advertising identifiers, ad interactions, and consent preferences.
  • Account identifiers and authentication information when you choose to use account or cloud save features.
  • Information you voluntarily provide when contacting us for support.

Some information is stored locally on your device, while other information may be processed by third-party services used by the game.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used for the following purposes:

  • Providing and maintaining our games.
  • Saving and restoring gameplay progress.
  • Supporting authentication and cloud synchronization.
  • Understanding game performance and improving gameplay.
  • Detecting technical problems, crashes, and service errors.
  • Displaying advertisements and, where permitted, measuring their effectiveness.
  • Processing purchases and restoring purchased content.
  • Responding to support requests.

3. Advertising

Some of our games display advertisements through Google AdMob and advertising partners participating in its mediation services.

Advertising providers may process information such as device identifiers, IP addresses, approximate location derived from network information, and advertising interactions.

Depending on your location, privacy choices, and applicable laws, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

Some games may offer optional rewarded advertisements that provide in-game rewards when viewed.

For more information about Google's advertising privacy practices, visit:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob Privacy Information

4. Analytics and Diagnostics

Some of our games may use analytics and diagnostic services, including Firebase services provided by Google.

These services may collect technical and usage information such as app interactions, session statistics, device information, and crash reports.

We use this information to understand how our games perform, investigate errors, and improve the user experience.

Learn more: Firebase Privacy Information

5. Accounts and Cloud Saves

Certain games may provide optional account authentication and cloud save functionality.

Depending on the game, authentication may be provided through services such as Google Play Games, Apple Sign-In, or Firebase Authentication.

When these features are used, we may process account identifiers and gameplay data necessary to associate saved progress with an account.

Cloud save data may include character progression, inventory, achievements, game settings, and other gameplay-related information.

Cloud data is used to synchronize progress, restore saved games, and maintain account-related features.

6. In-App Purchases

Some games offer optional in-app purchases. Payments are processed by platform providers such as Google Play and the Apple App Store.

We may receive purchase status, transaction identifiers, and other information needed to validate or restore purchased content.

Payment card details are processed by the relevant payment provider rather than directly by our games.

7. GDPR and Privacy Choices

Where required by applicable privacy laws, including those in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, our games may request consent for certain advertising and data processing activities.

We may use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to display consent messages and manage advertising privacy choices.

Where required, users can review or modify their advertising privacy choices through the Privacy Settings option available in the game.

You may also have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing, depending on applicable law.

8. Apple App Tracking Transparency

On iOS devices, our games may request permission to track activity across other companies' apps and websites through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework.

You can manage tracking permissions in your device's privacy settings.

Your choice regarding tracking is separate from other privacy choices that may be presented within the game.

9. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed through our games and services.

Depending on the feature, data may be stored locally on your device or processed by third-party infrastructure providers.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to applicable legal, security, and operational requirements.

Data stored locally on your device may be removed by deleting the application or clearing its data. This does not necessarily remove information stored in cloud services.

If a game provides an account or cloud save feature, you may contact us to request deletion of associated account information and saved data.

To request deletion, email us using the contact information below. Please include the name of the game and sufficient information to help us identify the relevant account. Do not send passwords or other sensitive authentication credentials.

We may need to verify account ownership before processing a request. Where applicable, we will also provide an in-game method to initiate account deletion.

Certain information may be retained where required by law or for legitimate purposes such as fraud prevention, security, or resolving transactions.

11. Third-Party Services

Depending on the game and platform, our games may integrate services provided by external companies, including:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob and its mediation partners
  • Google Firebase
  • Google Play Games Services
  • Apple Game Center and Apple Sign-In
  • Google Play Billing and Apple In-App Purchase services

These providers may process information under their own privacy policies. We encourage users to review the policies of relevant providers.

12. Children's Privacy

Our games are intended for audiences appropriate to their respective age ratings.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws.

If you believe that personal information relating to a child has been collected improperly, please contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

13. International Data Processing

Third-party service providers used by our games may process information in countries other than your country of residence.

Where applicable, such processing is subject to relevant legal requirements and safeguards.

14. Links to Other Websites

Our games may contain links to third-party websites or services that we do not operate.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those third-party websites. We recommend reviewing their respective privacy policies.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our games, services, or applicable legal requirements.

Updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Where required by law, we will provide additional notice.
